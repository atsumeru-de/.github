### 🌊 About Atsumeru

In Japanese, **Atsumeru (集める)** translates to *"to gather"*, *"to collect"*, or *"to assemble"*. 

Modern knowledge work is constantly fragmented by meetings, context switches, and transient thoughts. **Atsumeru** is an ecosystem of lightweight, friction-free productivity tools designed to capture your workday data in the background so you can stay completely in flow.

#### 🛠️ Ecosystem & Projects

- **[Toki (時)](https://toki.atsumeru.de)** — *Automatic Time & Activity Tracker*  
  Quiet background collector that monitors active windows, app usage, and focus periods—helping you understand where your time actually goes without manual stopwatches or timer anxiety.

- **[Omoi (思い)](https://omoi.atsumeru.de)** — *Frictionless Thought & Scratchpad Capture*  
  Instant global hotkey overlay for capturing fleeting thoughts, meeting notes, and action items in milliseconds, auto-linking notes to your active context and daily timeline.
